# Bountiful Trees v1.2.0

This update completely reworks Bountiful Trees with new visuals, improved tree behavior, and native compatibility with Bountiful Bonsais and UtilityCraft.

## HIGHLIGHTS
- Completely reworked all 12 resource trees.
- Added new textures, models, and growth animations.
- Added native compatibility with Bountiful Bonsais.
- Added native integration with UtilityCraft resources and progression.

---

## GAMEPLAY
- All Bountiful Trees saplings can now grow inside Bountiful Bonsais, with tier-based growth times and drops.
- Rebalanced tree costs, resource yields, and sapling drops across all tiers.
- Improved tree generation and natural leaf decay.
- Player-placed leaves remain persistent, and leaves no longer decay when nearby chunks are unavailable.
- Saplings now use a single item for placement, drops, and inventory selection.
